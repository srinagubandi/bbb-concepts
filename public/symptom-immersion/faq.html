Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you take the next step.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. The site provides general symptom-awareness information and lead intake only.

Why does the assessment start with symptoms?

Many visitors remember an ad because of snoring, fatigue, gasping, headaches, or poor sleep rather than because they already understand sleep apnea or oral appliance therapy.

What happens after I submit?

A participating provider assigned to the visitor's geographic territory would receive the qualified lead and follow up.

Will I be told which dentist I match with immediately?

No. The lead is captured first and the participating practice follows up.

Is Breathe Better Tonight tied to one appliance?

No. The provider program is appliance-agnostic and dentists retain clinical control.
