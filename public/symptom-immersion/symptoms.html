Symptoms first

Notice the pattern before you name it.

These signs can have many causes. They are useful reasons to start a conversation, not proof of a diagnosis.

Snoring

Frequent or disruptive snoring, especially when a partner notices changes in breathing.

Unrefreshing sleep

Sleeping for enough hours but still waking tired, foggy, or unrested.

Daytime fatigue

Sleepiness, concentration problems, or low energy during normal daily activities.

Gasping or choking

Sudden awakenings with a sensation that breathing was interrupted.

Morning headaches

Repeated headaches, dry mouth, or feeling poorly restored after sleep.

Observed pauses

A bed partner noticing pauses, irregular breathing, or abrupt resumption of breathing.

What happens next

A short assessment can organize what you are experiencing.

Breathe Better Tonight does not diagnose sleep apnea. The goal is to help a visitor recognize symptoms, provide contact information, and connect with a participating provider who can discuss an appropriate evaluation pathway.
