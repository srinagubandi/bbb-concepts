About the program

Make the first step into better sleep feel simpler.

Breathe Better Tonight is a consumer education and patient-acquisition brand designed to help people recognize sleep-related symptoms and connect with participating dental practices.

For patients

The experience starts with symptoms instead of treatment language. Visitors do not need to understand oral appliance therapy before asking for help.

For dentists

The provider program supports qualified patient acquisition, territory exclusivity, and conversion infrastructure while leaving clinical decision-making and appliance selection with the practice.
