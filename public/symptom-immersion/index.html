What if your sleep symptoms are connected?

Snoring. Waking tired. Gasping. Headaches. Daytime fatigue. Start with what you are experiencing, not with a diagnosis.

Common patterns

Different symptoms. One reason to pay attention.

01

Snoring

Loud or frequent snoring, especially when it disrupts someone else.

02

Daytime fatigue

Feeling sleepy, foggy, or low-energy despite spending enough time in bed.

03

Gasping or choking

Waking suddenly with a feeling that your breathing was interrupted.

04

Morning headaches

Repeated headaches or dry mouth when you wake up.
