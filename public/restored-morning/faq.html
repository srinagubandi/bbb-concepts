Frequently asked questions

Know what happens before you start.

Does this site diagnose sleep apnea?

No. It provides symptom-awareness information and a way to request follow-up from a participating provider.

Will treatment be discussed before I submit my information?

No. The initial journey is symptom-led. Treatment education follows lead capture.

Why do you ask about CPAP?

Only people who indicate a prior sleep apnea diagnosis are asked about CPAP experience, so a provider has useful context.

How is a provider selected?

The planned production model uses ZIP and proximity within an exclusive geographic territory.

What is the provider program?

A marketing and patient-acquisition program for dentists offering oral appliance therapy, with appliance choice and clinical decisions left to the practice.
