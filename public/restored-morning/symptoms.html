Start with the pattern

Five signs worth noticing.

Symptoms can have many causes. This page is designed to help you recognize patterns worth discussing with a qualified provider.

Snoring

Loud, frequent, or disruptive snoring.

Waking tired

Feeling unrefreshed after enough time in bed.

Gasping or choking

Sudden nighttime breathing interruptions.

Morning headaches

Recurring headaches or dry mouth after waking.

Daytime sleepiness

Reduced alertness, energy, or concentration.
