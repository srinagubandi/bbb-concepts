About Breathe Better Tonight

A consumer-first way to start a better sleep conversation.

This concept positions Breathe Better Tonight as a symptom-awareness and lead-capture brand for patients, with a separate growth program for participating dentists.

For patients

Recognize symptoms, answer a few simple questions, and request follow-up without being pushed toward a diagnosis or treatment before lead capture.

For providers

Participating dentists can use Breathe Better Tonight as a full-funnel patient-acquisition program with exclusive geographic territory positioning.
