Wake up feeling like yourself again

Better mornings can start with understanding your nights.

Snoring, waking tired, headaches, gasping, and daytime sleepiness can be easy to normalize. This experience helps people connect the dots and take a practical next step.

Common signs

Not one symptom. A pattern.

Snoring

Frequent or disruptive nighttime noise.

Waking tired

Feeling unrested despite enough time in bed.

Gasping or choking

Sudden nighttime breathing interruptions.

Morning headaches

A repeated clue worth discussing.

Daytime sleepiness

Low alertness, focus, or energy.

A low-friction start

Begin with what you feel, not a diagnosis.

The assessment starts with symptoms, then asks about insurance and any prior sleep apnea diagnosis. CPAP experience is only asked when relevant. Contact details come last.
