Symptom awareness

Common signs worth discussing.

These symptoms are not diagnostic by themselves. A qualified healthcare professional can determine what evaluation is appropriate.

Loud or frequent snoring

A recurring pattern that may be noticed most clearly by a bed partner.

Observed breathing pauses

Periods when breathing seems to stop or become irregular during sleep.

Waking gasping or choking

Sudden awakenings with a breathless or choking sensation.

Unrefreshing sleep

Getting enough time in bed but still waking tired or foggy.

Daytime sleepiness

Difficulty staying alert, focused, or energized during the day.

Morning headaches or dry mouth

Symptoms on waking that can add context to an ongoing sleep concern.
