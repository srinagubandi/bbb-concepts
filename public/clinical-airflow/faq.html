Frequently asked questions

What visitors and providers need to know.

Does the site diagnose sleep apnea?

No. It provides symptom awareness and a lead-intake pathway.

Why not explain oral appliances before lead capture?

The approved strategy is symptom-first. Treatment education appears after the visitor has asked for help.

How are patients matched?

By ZIP/proximity into the participating dentist's exclusive geographic territory.

Do patients see the dentist immediately?

No. The lead is captured first and the assigned participating practice follows up.

Are dentists required to use a specific appliance?

No. The program is appliance-agnostic.

Are lead volumes guaranteed?

No. HCP messaging focuses on qualified patient opportunities without guaranteed volume, revenue, or ROI.
